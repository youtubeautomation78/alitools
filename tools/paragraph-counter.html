About This Paragraph Counter

Long walls of text lose readers. This free paragraph counter shows exactly how your writing is structured: how many paragraphs you have, how many sentences, how many words — plus the average words per paragraph and per sentence, so you can spot paragraphs that are too long and sentences that are hard to read. Counts update live as you type or edit.

How to Use It

  1. Paste your essay, article or document into the box.
  2. See paragraph, sentence, word and character counts instantly.
  3. Check the averages: very long paragraphs (150+ words) usually need splitting.
  4. Edit your text right in the box and watch the numbers improve.

Good Writing Benchmarks

  • Paragraph length — 50 to 150 words reads well on screens.
  • Sentence length — an average of 15 to 20 words keeps readers engaged.
  • Blog posts — short paragraphs with white space increase reading time.
