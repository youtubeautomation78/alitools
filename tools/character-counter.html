About This Character Counter

A character counter tells you exactly how long a piece of text is. That matters more than most people expect: a post on X is capped at 280 characters, an Instagram bio at 150, and Google usually shows only about 155 characters of a meta description before cutting it off. Going over by even a few characters can truncate a headline or stop a form from submitting.

This counter shows the total characters (including spaces and line breaks), the count without spaces, the number of words and the number of lines. Everything is calculated in your browser, so private drafts and client copy never leave your device.

How to Use It

  1. Type or paste your text into the live counter box.
  2. Read the character, no-space, word and line counts as they update.
  3. Compare the total with the limit for the platform you are writing for (see the table below).
  4. Edit until you are under the limit, then copy your text.
