About This Word Counter Tool

Our free online word counter helps writers, students, bloggers and professionals keep their text within limits. Whether you are writing an essay with a strict word count, a tweet with a character limit, a meta description for SEO, or a speech with a time limit, this tool gives you every number you need in real time — words, characters (with and without spaces), sentences, paragraphs, reading time and speaking time.

Unlike many counters, everything runs directly in your browser. Your text is never sent to a server, so private documents, unpublished articles and client work stay completely confidential.

How to Use the Word Counter

  1. Type directly into the box, or paste your text from any document or website.
  2. Watch the counters update live as you type — no button press needed.
  3. Check words, characters, sentences, paragraphs and estimated reading time.
  4. Click Copy Text to copy your text, or Clear to start again.

Who Is This Tool For?

  • Students — keep essays and assignments inside the required word limit.
  • Bloggers & SEOs — check article length, titles and meta descriptions.
  • Social media managers — stay within platform character limits.
  • Speakers & YouTubers — estimate script reading and speaking time.
