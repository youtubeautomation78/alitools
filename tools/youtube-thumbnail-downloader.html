About This Tool

Need a YouTube thumbnail for a blog post, presentation, reaction video or design reference? This free downloader grabs the official thumbnail images of any public YouTube video. Paste the video link and get every available size — full HD (1280×720), SD (640×480), HQ (480×360) and MQ (320×180) — ready to view and save. It works with normal videos, youtu.be short links, Shorts and even just the video ID.

How to Use It

  1. Copy the URL of any public YouTube video.
  2. Paste it into the box and click Get Thumbnails.
  3. Preview all four sizes with their resolutions.
  4. Click Open / Save under the size you want, then save the image to your device.

Useful Tips

  • HD (1280×720) exists only if the uploader added a high-resolution thumbnail or the video is HD.
  • Use thumbnails for references, embeds and fair-use commentary — remember thumbnails belong to their creators.
  • Creators: study competitor thumbnails to improve your own click-through rate.
