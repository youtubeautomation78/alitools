About This Tool

The comma separator converts a vertical list — one item per line — into a single comma-separated line. It is the fastest way to prepare keyword lists, email address lists, product tags, spreadsheet data and programming arrays. You can also switch the separator to semicolons, pipes or spaces, sort the items alphabetically, and remove duplicates automatically.

How to Use It

  1. Paste your list into the box (one item per line, or items separated by spaces).
  2. Pick your separator: comma, comma + space, semicolon, pipe or space.
  3. Optionally remove duplicates and sort A–Z.
  4. Click Copy Result and paste the finished list anywhere.

Common Uses

  • SEO — prepare keyword and meta tag lists.
  • Marketing — build email and audience lists.
  • E-commerce — format product tags and categories.
  • Development — turn lines into arrays and CSV-style data.
