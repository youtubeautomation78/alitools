About This Tool

Text copied from PDFs, emails and websites often arrives full of double spaces, tabs and random blank lines. This free tool cleans it instantly. It collapses repeated spaces into one, trims stray spaces from the start and end of every line, converts tabs to spaces, and removes stacks of empty lines — while keeping your real paragraphs intact.

How to Use It

  1. Paste your messy text into the input box.
  2. Choose the cleaning options you need (all are on by default).
  3. Your clean text appears instantly in the result box — the counter shows how many characters were removed.
  4. Click Copy Result and use your clean text anywhere.

Who Needs This?

  • Writers & editors — clean drafts copied from Word or Google Docs.
  • Data entry workers — fix lists and records before importing them.
  • Developers — tidy pasted text, logs and content files.
  • Students — clean copied research notes and references.
