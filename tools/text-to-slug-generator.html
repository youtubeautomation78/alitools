About This Slug Generator

A slug is the readable part of a URL — for example, my-first-blog-post in example.com/my-first-blog-post. Clean slugs rank better, look trustworthy and get more clicks. This free generator converts any title into a perfect slug: it lowercases the text, removes accents and special characters, replaces spaces with hyphens (or underscores), and trims everything messy — instantly, as you type.

How to Use It

  1. Type or paste your page title or any text.
  2. Choose hyphens (recommended by Google for SEO) or underscores.
  3. Your slug updates live — click Copy Slug to grab it.
  4. Paste it into WordPress, Blogger, Shopify or any CMS permalink field.

Slug Best Practices

  • Keep slugs short — 3 to 5 words is ideal.
  • Include your main keyword, remove filler words like "the" and "a" if the slug gets long.
  • Always use lowercase and hyphens; never use spaces or capital letters in URLs.
