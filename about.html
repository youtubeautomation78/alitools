About AliSuperTools

Simple, powerful online tools that respect your time — and your privacy.

Our Story

AliSuperTools started with a simple frustration: small everyday tasks — counting words in an essay, fixing capitalization, sorting a list, grabbing a YouTube thumbnail — kept sending us to slow websites full of pop-ups, forced sign-ups and download buttons that were not real download buttons.

So we built the tools website we always wanted: one place where every tool opens instantly, works the moment you start typing, and never asks for your email address. No accounts. No uploads. No nonsense.

What We Do

We build free browser-based tools for writers, students, teachers, bloggers, YouTubers, marketers and developers. Our current collection includes a word counter, paragraph counter, sentence case converter, comma separator, extra space remover, alphabetical text organizer, URL slug generator and a YouTube thumbnail downloader — and we add new tools regularly based on what our users ask for.

Our Mission

To make useful tools free and accessible to everyone, everywhere — on any device, in any country, without barriers. If a tool can run in your browser, we believe you should never have to pay for it, register for it, or hand over your data to use it.

Why People Choose AliSuperTools

  • Truly free — every tool is free forever, with no premium version hiding the good features.
  • Private by design — tools run 100% in your browser. Your text is never uploaded to or stored on our servers.
  • Instant results — most tools work in real time as you type. No submit buttons, no waiting.
  • Works everywhere — phone, tablet, laptop or desktop, our tools adapt to your screen.
  • Beginner friendly — clean design and clear instructions on every tool page, including FAQs.

How We Keep It Free

AliSuperTools is supported by advertising. Ads allow us to keep every tool free for everyone while we continue building new ones. We work hard to keep ads from ever interfering with the tools themselves.

Get in Touch

Have an idea for a new tool? Found a bug? Want to say hello? We read every message — visit our Contact page and drop us a line. User requests directly shape what we build next.
