Creators often want to save a thumbnail to study its design, keep a reference, or recover their own artwork. Here is how to download a YouTube thumbnail in the best quality available.

## Steps to download a thumbnail

1. Open the YouTube video and copy its link from the address bar or the Share button.
2. Open the [YouTube Thumbnail Downloader](../tools/youtube-thumbnail-downloader.html).
3. Paste the link into the box. Watch links, youtu.be links and Shorts links all work.
4. Choose the size you want and download the image.

## What sizes are available?

The largest thumbnail YouTube serves is **1280 × 720 pixels** (16:9). YouTube also generates smaller versions such as 640 × 480, 480 × 360 and 320 × 180. The HD version only exists when the uploader provided a high-resolution thumbnail, so older or low-resolution videos may offer only the smaller sizes.

## Using thumbnails fairly

Thumbnails belong to the person who made the video. Downloading one for personal reference or learning is generally fine, but you should not re-upload someone else's thumbnail as your own or use it commercially without permission. If you want to reuse your own thumbnail, download it and keep the original source file somewhere safe next time.

## Tips for better thumbnails

- Use a clear focal point and big, readable text.
- Keep the key content away from the bottom-right corner, where the video length badge appears.
- Test how the thumbnail looks small, because most people see it on a phone.
- Use high contrast so it stands out in a crowded feed.
