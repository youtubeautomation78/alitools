What Tools Do We Offer?

Ali Super Tools is a free online toolbox for people who work with words and content every day. Every tool runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored, and there is no sign-up wall between you and the result. Below is what you will find here and who each tool is for.

Text and Writing Tools

Most of the toolbox is built for writing. The Word Counter Generator counts words, characters (with and without spaces), sentences and paragraphs as you type, and adds reading time, speaking time, keyword density and readability scores — the checks students need for essay limits and writers need for briefs. The Paragraph Counter breaks long drafts into paragraphs and averages, while the Sentence Case Generator fixes capitalization in one click, including UPPER CASE, Title Case and aLtErNaTiNg case. Cleaning text is covered too: the Extra Spaces Remover strips double spaces and empty lines, the Comma Separator turns lists into comma-separated values for spreadsheets and tags, and the Alphabetical Text Organizer sorts lines A–Z, by length or in reverse. If you publish online, the Slug Generator turns any headline into a clean, SEO-friendly URL.

YouTube Tools

For creators, the YouTube Thumbnail Downloader grabs the HD thumbnail (1280×720) from any public video or Shorts link — useful for studying what makes thumbnails get clicked, reusing your own artwork, or saving a reference for your next design. Paste the link, pick the size, download the image. No extension and no watermark.
