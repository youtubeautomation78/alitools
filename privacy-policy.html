Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Ali Super Tools ("we", "our", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit alitools-nine.vercel.app (the "Website"), how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this Website, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide

Our tools do not require registration or an account. If you contact us by email, we receive your name, email address and the content of your message. We use this information only to respond to you. Messages sent through the contact form are delivered to our email inbox via FormSubmit, a third-party form-processing service, whose handling of your message is covered by its own privacy policy.

Your tool data

Nearly all tools on this Website run entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Text and content you paste into the tools is processed on your own device and is never uploaded to, transmitted to, or stored on our servers.

Log files and analytics

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record standard log information such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and the date and time of your visit. This data is used in aggregate to understand how the Website is used and to keep it secure.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

This Website uses cookies. A cookie is a small file stored on your device by your browser. We and our partners use cookies to:

  • Make the Website work properly and remember preferences;
  • Understand how visitors use the Website (analytics);
  • Serve and measure advertising, as described in the next section.

You can disable cookies at any time in your browser settings. If you disable cookies, some features of the Website may not work as intended, but all tools will remain usable.

4. Google AdSense and Advertising Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on this Website. Please note the following:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this Website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Users may also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices.
  • For users in the European Economic Area, Google may show a consent message before serving personalized ads, in line with its EU user consent policy.

You can learn more about how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

5. Third-Party Privacy Policies

This Privacy Policy applies only to this Website. It does not apply to advertisers or other websites that we link to. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites or ad servers you visit. We have no control over, and assume no responsibility for, the content or practices of third-party services.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the limited information we handle. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Information

Ali Super Tools does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided such information on our Website, please contact us and we will promptly remove it.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold about you (for example, emails you have sent us). To exercise any of these rights, contact us through the Contact page and we will respond within a reasonable time.
